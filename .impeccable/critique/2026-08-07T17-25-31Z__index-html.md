## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Genuinely authored, not template. Service architecture maps to a real San Juan solo babysitter (tourists, destination weddings, resorts), PR-native register, real credentials, mostly authentic photos of real children and Amanda. Layout grammar is standard landing-page fare and two service photos are visibly stock — the skeleton shows at the edges, but content specificity carries it.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI: 1 finding — false positive (`bounce-easing` matched the token name `--animate-bounce-slow`, actual easing is `ease-in-out`). In-page detector: 20 findings per locale, identical on ES and EN: 16 low-contrast pairs (orange #fc7e38 on cream 2.4:1; blue/green accents 3.6:1; #afafaf meta text 2.0–2.2:1; white on orange cards 2.6:1; white on the hero CTA blue 3.8:1), 3 clipped-overflow containers (decorative elements intentionally clipped — benign), 1 cream-palette note (the brand's pinned background — false positive). Where A and B agree: the contrast failures on brand-colored text are real and measured independently by both.

**Visual overlays:** rendered and screenshot-verified during assessment in both locales; the overlay server was stopped afterward, so they are no longer live. (One /en/ injection failed due to cross-assessment tab interference in the shared browser; an isolated-context rerun confirmed /en/ stays English — site behavior is correct.)

## Overall Impression

A warm, genuinely authored page whose biggest asset is authenticity (real kids, real face, real degrees) and whose biggest gap is that the trust content parents screen for — safety, supervision, certifications, testimonials — is either hidden behind carousels or absent entirely. The mechanics are strong; the persuasion architecture is one layer short.

## What's Working

1. **Authentic photography as a trust engine** — hero collage and first two service slides are recognizably her real charges; About portrait is real. In a "trust me with your child" category this beats polished stock.
2. **Bilingual market fit is structural** — ES default with /en/ URLs, auto-redirect by browser language, hotel/tourist service aimed at the EN segment, equally idiomatic copy in both.
3. **Form scaffolding** — "Nombres y edades (ej. Emma 5, Lucas 3)" placeholder, correct mobile keyboards, 44px submit, explicit success state. The highest-stakes interaction is the best-engineered one.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Hidden trust content in desktop carousels** — Activities shows 4 of 8 cards with no indicator; "Seguridad y Supervisión" and "Apoyo Emocional" are effectively invisible. Fix: 4×2 grid on lg+; visible dots/tabs for services. *(/impeccable layout)*
2. **[P1] Measured contrast failures on brand-colored text** — orange-on-cream 2.36:1, pink active nav 2.11:1, #afafaf meta text 2.19:1, white body on orange card 2.57:1. Fix: darker text-role variants of brand hues (keep bright fills for shapes); replace #afafaf in text roles. *(/impeccable colorize)*
3. **[P1] CTA promises booking; the form can't book** — no date/time field anywhere; also "Tipo de Servicio*" required-vs-optional mismatch. Fix: add optional date/schedule field or reframe CTA as inquiry; align select with schema. *(/impeccable clarify + harden)*
4. **[P2] Mobile carousel affordances** — 8×8px dots (44px minimum), ~200px dead space from min-h-105, swipe cue is a tiny "1/4". Fix: peek next slide, enlarge dot hit areas, drop mobile min-height. *(/impeccable adapt)*
5. **[P2] Nothing at the ask answers the #1 parent question** — no CPR/first-aid/background-check mention, no testimonial despite "100+ familias". Fix: credentials strip above the form + 1–2 parent quotes (requires facts from the owner). *(/impeccable delight/clarify — content needed)*

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** takes "4 activity cards" literally and misses safety content; believes the service asterisk; hits the form ready to book and finds no date field. Good: labeled icons, explicit success state, no jargon.

**Riley (stress tester):** required-UI/optional-schema mismatch; empty submit doesn't scroll/focus first error (off-screen on mobile); language switch loses scroll position and typed form data; failure message says "contact me directly" without a link; "100+ familias" has zero verifiable evidence.

**Casey (mobile):** email field missing `autoComplete="email"`; 8px carousel dots; swipe-only service discovery means she may never learn hotel babysitting exists; no persistent WhatsApp button in a WhatsApp-first market. Good: CTA in first viewport, no horizontal overflow, lazy images, 44px form controls.

## Minor Observations

- No `prefers-reduced-motion` handling (MotionConfig).
- No skip-to-content link; input focus indication faint (1px border swap, ring suppressed).
- Unused multi-MB original images still ship in public/assets/images (deploy weight).
- bio2 is a verbless resume fragment; dark #1a1a1a card is a tonal outlier; footer is a dead end (no repeated contact/nav).
- Heading hierarchy clean (one h1, h2 sections, h3 cards); alt text meaningful; ES/EN aria-labels correctly bilingual.

## Questions to Consider

1. The page's strongest evidence and weakest coverage are the same topic — trust. Why is the one thing every parent screens for the one thing never stated plainly?
2. Is this a booking page or a lead-gen page? CTA, headline, and form disagree.
3. If PR parents would rather WhatsApp than fill a six-field form, should tap-to-WhatsApp be the persistent primary action with the form as the tourist-friendly fallback?
